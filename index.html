FAQ

Common questions

Do I still pay Meta directly?

No — m3vo routes your messages through an official Business Solution Provider, so you get one invoice. We itemize the Meta pass-through cost on it instead of folding it into an unmarked "platform fee."

What happens after October 1, 2026, when service messages start being charged?

Meta's per-message rate for service and utility replies inside the 24-hour window becomes chargeable on that date across the entire category. m3vo passes that fee through at cost, same as today — it will not be absorbed into a higher subscription price without being shown to you first.

Is this built on an unofficial WhatsApp library?

No. m3vo connects exclusively through an official BSP on the WhatsApp Cloud API. Unofficial libraries carry real account-ban risk, which isn't acceptable for a product businesses depend on.

Can I import my contacts and history from another platform?

Yes — CSV import today, with direct migration tooling for the platforms above on the roadmap.
