What is M3VO?

A shared WhatsApp and omnichannel inbox for your whole team.

M3VO lets multiple team members manage customer conversations from one workspace. Businesses use M3VO to:

  • Receive and reply to WhatsApp messages

  • Assign conversations to specific team members

  • Add internal notes for team collaboration

  • Organise conversations by status

  • Track customer enquiries and follow-ups

  • Create automated messaging workflows

  • Use AI assistance to prepare faster replies

  • Maintain visibility across customer-facing teams

  • Manage multiple WhatsApp numbers from one platform

  • Connect customer conversations with existing business systems

M3VO is built for businesses that have outgrown the standard WhatsApp Business app and need better control, accountability, and automation.

FAQ

Common questions

What is M3VO?

M3VO is a shared WhatsApp inbox and customer communication platform. It brings customer conversations into one workspace so multiple team members can receive, assign, manage, and reply to them without losing context.

Is M3VO an official WhatsApp Business solution?

Yes. M3VO connects through the official WhatsApp Business Platform using an approved Business Solution Provider (BSP). It does not depend on unofficial WhatsApp Web automation or unsupported messaging libraries that risk your account.

Can multiple employees use the same WhatsApp number?

Yes. Authorised team members can manage conversations tied to a connected business number through the shared M3VO inbox, with assignment, internal notes, and status tracking so nothing gets duplicated or missed.

Can M3VO manage multiple WhatsApp numbers?

Yes. M3VO is built to support businesses with multiple numbers, teams, departments, or locations. Availability depends on your plan — see the pricing section for what's included at each tier.

Does M3VO include AI tools?

Yes. M3VO's AI copilot helps staff draft replies, summarise conversations, and qualify leads faster. It assists your team rather than replacing human judgement — staff stay in control of what gets sent.

Does M3VO support automation?

Yes. You can automate routine processes — greetings, enquiry routing, reminders, follow-ups, and status updates — with a no-code flow builder included from the Starter plan.

Is M3VO only for large companies?

No. M3VO is built for growing small and medium-sized businesses as well as larger teams that need structured WhatsApp communication — pricing is per seat, so it scales with your team rather than penalising growth.

Is M3VO a CRM?

M3VO includes CRM-style functionality — a lightweight pipeline view, contact profiles, assignment, and communication history — alongside the shared inbox, without forcing you into a separate tool.

Which businesses can use M3VO?

M3VO is used by clinics, dental and aesthetic practices, retailers and e-commerce businesses, professional service providers, education and training providers, and other organisations that manage customer conversations through WhatsApp.

How is M3VO priced?

M3VO uses transparent, per-seat subscription pricing based on your team size and the features you need, not the size of your contact list. Meta and BSP messaging fees are itemized separately at cost, never marked up.

Can I try M3VO before subscribing?

Yes — contact the M3VO team at hello@m3vo.com to request access, arrange a walkthrough, or ask about your specific use case.

What happens after October 1, 2026, when service messages start being charged?

Meta's per-message rate for service and utility replies inside the 24-hour window becomes chargeable on that date across the entire category. M3VO passes that fee through at cost, same as today — it will not be absorbed into a higher subscription price without being shown to you first.
