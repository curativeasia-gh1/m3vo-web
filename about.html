About M3VO

We think a WhatsApp inbox shouldn't cost five times what the messages actually cost.

M3VO is built by Mevo Tech, a project by Curative Holdings. We started it after mapping out what respond.io, Wati, SleekFlow, Kommo, and the rest of the WhatsApp Business Platform category actually charge for — and finding that almost none of it is the WhatsApp connection itself.

Meta charges the same per-message rate to every vendor in this category. The 5–10x difference in what businesses actually pay isn't the messaging — it's the wrapper around it.

— The M3VO team

Every "WhatsApp Business Platform" product on the market — respond.io, Wati, SleekFlow, Kommo, Trengo, Interakt, AiSensy, Gallabox — licenses access to the same Meta Cloud API through the same kind of Business Solution Provider. None of them own the messaging pipe. Meta sets the per-message cost, and every vendor stacks a subscription fee, and often a messaging markup, on top of an identical commodity.

What differs — wildly — is how much of that stack gets shown to the customer. Automation and AI gated behind a $129+ tier. Pricing that taxes you for every contact your business grows to reach. Messaging fees folded into an unmarked "platform fee" instead of itemized. We built M3VO to remove that gap between what a WhatsApp inbox costs to run and what it's sold for.

M3VO ships a shared inbox, no-code automation, and an AI copilot in every plan that needs them — not just the top one — priced per seat instead of per contact, with Meta and BSP fees itemized on every invoice at cost.

What we believe

Four rules we hold ourselves to.

Transparency by default

Every invoice itemizes the subscription, the BSP fee, and the Meta pass-through separately. Nothing gets folded into an unmarked "platform fee."

Seats, never a contact tax

You shouldn't pay more because your customer list grew. Price scales with the size of your team, not with how many people you've talked to.

Automation and AI aren't premium add-ons

They ship in the plans that actually need them — not gated behind the most expensive tier, the way most of this category prices them.

Official channels only

M3VO connects through a licensed WhatsApp Business Solution Provider — never an unofficial library that puts your account at ban risk.
