Many businesses rely on WhatsApp every day but still manage customer conversations manually. Messages get handled on personal devices, forwarded between team members, or left unanswered because nobody knows who's responsible. As the business grows, this creates duplicated replies, missed enquiries, slow follow-ups, and limited visibility for managers.

Existing WhatsApp Business Platform solutions don't make this easier. Subscription fees, messaging fees, provider charges, and optional features are frequently bundled together or restricted to higher-priced plans — and every vendor in the category licenses the same underlying Meta Cloud API, so the difference in price rarely reflects a difference in the messaging itself.

M3VO gives growing businesses the essential tools to manage WhatsApp communication professionally: a shared inbox, no-code automation, and an AI copilot in every plan that needs them, priced per seat instead of per contact, with Meta and BSP fees itemized on every invoice at cost.

Our approach

Our approach to WhatsApp business software.

Transparency by default

Our pricing and invoices clearly separate the different components of the service — the M3VO subscription and any applicable external messaging or provider charges.

Your growth should not be penalised

A growing contact list is a positive sign. We don't believe businesses should pay more simply because they've spoken to more customers — pricing is based on team access, not contact count.

Useful features should be accessible

Shared inbox management, automation, and AI support are practical operational tools, not decorative extras. We make them available in the plans where businesses genuinely need them.

Official channels only

M3VO is built for long-term, professional use. We support official WhatsApp Business Platform connections rather than unofficial workarounds.

Technology should support people

Automation and AI work best when they remove repetitive tasks and give employees more time for meaningful customer interactions. M3VO keeps people at the centre of the process.

Beyond the inbox

The WhatsApp inbox is where we started. It's not where the problem ends.

Once conversations are organised, the next bottleneck is usually somewhere else — leads that aren't tracked, follow-ups that depend on someone remembering, spreadsheets standing in for a real system. The same team behind M3VO works with growing businesses on the systems around the inbox, using whatever mix of automation, AI and integration actually fits the problem.

CRM & sales management

Organise leads, quotations and customer records in one place, with follow-ups that don't depend on memory.

Business process automation

Reminders, report prep, task assignment and data transfer handled automatically, so time goes to customers instead of admin.

Applied AI

AI aimed at specific jobs — enquiry triage, lead qualification, document processing, internal knowledge — not features for their own sake.

System integration

Connecting the accounting, CRM and operational tools already in use, so the same information isn't typed in twice.

Custom builds

For workflows off-the-shelf software can't cover, we design and build tailored tools and dashboards.
